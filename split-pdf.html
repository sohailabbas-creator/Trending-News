About Split PDF

PDF viewer page numbers can differ from printed labels. Note the required pages before using a split or extract command, especially when choosing several non-consecutive ranges or handling sensitive records.

Steps for Split PDF

  1. Select the PDF and identify the pages to keep.
  2. Enter page numbers or ranges, such as 1-3, 5.
  3. Choose Extract pages and download the new PDF.
  4. Open the result and verify its page count and sequence.

Review the result

Confirm no neighboring page was included by mistake and that each output opens correctly. Retain the source until recipients have confirmed they can use the extracted documents.

Frequently asked questions

Does splitting remove pages from the original?

Most tools save the selected pages as new files and leave the source unchanged. Check the application's save behavior and preserve a backup.

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Many tools accept multiple page numbers or ranges. Follow the tool's range syntax and check that output pages appear in the intended order.
