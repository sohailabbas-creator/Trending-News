What the Age Calculator does

Age is not simply days divided by 365.25. Official forms ask for age in completed years on a specific date, and a birthday that falls on 29 February needs a rule. Someone born on 29 February 2000 is 24 on 28 February 2024 in most legal systems, and 25 on 1 March, depending on the jurisdiction.

This calculator counts complete years, then complete months, then remaining days, which is the method used on application forms. It also gives totals in weeks, days and hours, and counts down to the next birthday, so it works for visa applications, school entry dates and HR records as well as curiosity.

How to use it

  1. Enter the date of birth.
  2. Leave the second date as today, or pick the date you need the age on — an exam date, a travel date or a contract start.
  3. Read the age in completed years, months and days.
  4. Use the totals below for weeks, days and hours, and check the birthday countdown.

Features

  • Age on any future or past date

Frequently asked questions

How is age calculated exactly?

Complete years first, then complete months, then remaining days. Someone born on 15 June 1995 is 31 on 15 June 2026 and remains 31 years and some months until 15 June 2027. This matches how age is recorded on official forms.

What about people born on 29 February?

In most legal systems someone born on 29 February reaches a new age on 28 February or 1 March in non-leap years, depending on local law. This calculator advances the anniversary to 1 March when 29 February does not exist.

Why is my age in days different on another site?

Usually because the other site counts the birth date itself as a full day, or ignores leap days. We count real calendar days between the two dates, including every 29 February.

Can I calculate age for a pet?

Yes, in the same way as a person, but note that animal ageing is not linear: a dog's first year is roughly equivalent to 15 human years, not one. Use a species-specific chart for health decisions.
