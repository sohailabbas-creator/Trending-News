What the Date Duration Calculator does

Counting days between two dates sounds trivial until you hit a month boundary, a leap year or a contract clause about whether the start date counts. A notice period of 30 days from 15 January ends on 14 February, not 15 February, if the start day is excluded. Ten working days is two calendar weeks only if there are no public holidays in between.

This calculator gives the duration in years, months and days as well as the raw total in days, weeks and hours, and it can count weekdays only. You choose whether the end date is included, because that single choice changes the answer by a day and is the most common source of disputes.

How to use it

  1. Pick the start date and the end date.
  2. Choose whether to include the end date in the count.
  3. Switch to business days if you need working days rather than calendar days.
  4. Read the breakdown in years, months and days alongside the raw day and week totals.

Features

  • Duration in years, months and days plus raw totals
  • Optional exclusion of the end date
  • Weekday-only counting for deadlines
  • Handles leap years and differing month lengths correctly
  • Reverse direction for past dates and countdowns

Worked examples

Notice period

30 days from 15 January, excluding the start date, ends on 14 February — 30 calendar days later. If the contract counts the start date as day one, the notice ends on 13 February.

Working days

From 1 March to 31 March there are 31 calendar days, but only 21 weekdays in a typical year. Use the business-day mode when a deadline says ten working days.

Frequently asked questions

Should the start date be counted?

It depends on the context. Legal notice periods usually start the day after notice is given, so the start date is excluded. Interest calculations and hotel stays often count nights, not days. This calculator lets you choose, because guessing wrong shifts the answer by a day.

How are working days counted?

Weekdays are Monday to Friday, excluding Saturday and Sunday. Public holidays are not deducted automatically because they differ by country and even by region — check your national holiday calendar if a deadline is critical.

Why does the month total sometimes look odd?

Months have 28 to 31 days, so a month-based duration is an approximation of calendar months. From 31 January, one month later is 28 or 29 February, not 31 February. The years-months-days breakdown avoids this by using real calendar boundaries.

Does this handle leap years?

Yes. 29 February is counted in leap years, so February has 29 days and any span crossing it includes the extra day.
