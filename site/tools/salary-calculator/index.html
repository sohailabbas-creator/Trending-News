What the Salary Calculator does

An annual salary and an hourly rate are not comparable until you agree on how many hours a year you actually work. A 40-hour week over 52 weeks is 2,080 hours; a UK-style 37.5-hour week is 1,950; subtract five weeks of holiday and unpaid breaks and a 60,000 salary can sit anywhere between about 25.60 and 32.90 an hour. That gap is the reason two people can quote different hourly figures for the same job.

This converter works in both directions and lets you set the number of paid hours per week and paid weeks per year, so the hourly figure reflects your real contract rather than a default. It converts pay in gross terms only — income tax, national insurance and social contributions are deliberately left out, because those rules differ by country and by personal circumstance.

How to use it

  1. Choose the direction: annual salary to hourly, or hourly rate to salary.
  2. Enter the amount you know.
  3. Set paid hours per week and paid weeks per year — the defaults are 40 and 52, which is 2,080 hours.
  4. Read the hourly, weekly, monthly and annual equivalents together, then copy the one you need.

Features

  • Converts in both directions: salary to hourly and hourly to salary
  • Configurable paid hours per week and paid weeks per year
  • Shows the implied annual working hours so the maths is auditable
  • Handles part-time and pro rata hours
  • Explicitly excludes tax, so figures are gross and comparable

Worked examples

60,000 a year at 40 hours, 52 weeks

60,000 ÷ (40 × 52) = 60,000 ÷ 2,080 = 28.85 an hour. Monthly pay is 5,000 and weekly pay is 1,153.85.

The same salary with real working weeks

With 46 paid weeks (six weeks of holiday and unpaid breaks), 60,000 ÷ (40 × 46) = 32.61 an hour. The salary has not changed, but the hourly value has, which is why contractors price differently from employees.

Frequently asked questions

How many working hours are in a year?

A 40-hour week over 52 weeks is 2,080 hours, which is the standard US figure. A 37.5-hour UK week gives 1,950. If you take unpaid holiday or unpaid breaks, subtract those hours — the calculator lets you set paid weeks per year for exactly this reason.

Is this a take-home pay calculator?

No. It converts gross pay between periods. Income tax, national insurance, social security and pension contributions depend on where you live and your personal situation, so applying a generic deduction would produce a misleading number. Use your employer's payroll or your national tax office calculator for net pay.

What does pro rata mean?

Pro rata means in proportion. A 30,000 full-time salary worked at 22.5 hours a week instead of 37.5 becomes 30,000 × (22.5 ÷ 37.5) = 18,000. Enter the part-time hours and the full-time salary to see the equivalent.

Should holiday be included in the hourly rate?

For employees, paid holiday is included in the salary, so use the weeks you are paid for, which is usually 52. For freelancers and contractors, holiday is unpaid, so use 46 to 48 working weeks to build a realistic day rate.

Why is my payslip hourly rate different from this figure?

Payroll often uses a fixed divisor, such as 2,080 hours or 26 fortnights, rather than your actual hours, and may include allowances or overtime in the base. Check which divisor your employer uses before comparing.
