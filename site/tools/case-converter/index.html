What the Case Converter does

Text arrives in the wrong case constantly: a headline in all caps from a spreadsheet, a variable name that should be snake_case, a sentence that lost its capital letter after an edit. Retyping is slow and error-prone, especially across several paragraphs.

This converter handles nine styles, including the three naming conventions developers need — camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case — and preserves line breaks so whole documents can be converted without losing their structure. Undo steps back through your conversions in order.

How to use it

  1. Paste the text you want to change.
  2. Click the case style you need. The result appears immediately.
  3. Check the line breaks and punctuation survived — they do, by design.
  4. Copy the result, or press Undo to go back a step.

Features

  • Nine styles: UPPER, lower, Title, Sentence, camel, Pascal, snake, kebab and CONSTANT
  • Title case keeps small words like and, of and the lowercase
  • Preserves paragraphs and line breaks
  • Undo history for each conversion
  • One-click copy

Worked examples

Title case done properly

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog becomes The Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over the Lazy Dog — Jumps, Over and The are capitalised, but over stays lowercase because it is a short preposition.

Headline to variable name

User Login Timeout in Seconds becomes userLoginTimeoutInSeconds in camelCase, user_login_timeout_in_seconds in snake_case and user-login-timeout-in-seconds in kebab-case.

Frequently asked questions

What is Title Case?

Title case capitalises the first and last words and all major words, while leaving short articles, conjunctions and prepositions lowercase unless they come first. So: The Man Who Knew Too Much, not The Man who Knew too Much.

Which case should I use for URLs and file names?

kebab-case for URLs and CSS class names, because search engines treat hyphens as word separators. snake_case is conventional for database columns, JSON keys and Python variables. Avoid spaces in any of them.

What is Sentence case?

Sentence case capitalises only the first letter of each sentence and any proper nouns — the style used by most British newspapers and the GOV.UK design system. It is easier to read than title case for long headings.

Does converting case change the words?

No. Only letter case changes, except in camel, Pascal, snake, kebab and constant modes, which also replace spaces with formatting characters and remove punctuation as part of building a valid identifier.
