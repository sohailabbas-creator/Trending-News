PDF privacy guide

Redact PDF

Redaction is intended to permanently remove selected content before sharing. Drawing a black box or highlight over words may leave the original text in the file.

Guide page, not an active tool: Sohail Tools does not currently process or export files. Use a trusted PDF application to follow these steps.

About Redact PDF

A dedicated redaction process removes selected content from a saved copy. Sensitive information may also appear in repeated text, scanned images, comments, attachments, or metadata.

Steps for Redact PDF

  1. Identify every occurrence of the information, including headers and metadata.
  2. Use software designed for redaction rather than a drawing or highlight tool.
  3. Apply changes to a separate copy and close the editor after saving.
  4. Reopen the result and search, select, or copy text to verify removal.

Review the result

Inspect affected pages, comments, attachments, and OCR text. Follow retention rules for the original and have a second reviewer check high-impact documents when appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

Is drawing a black rectangle enough to redact text?

No. A shape may cover text visually while leaving it selectable or searchable. Use a dedicated redaction feature and verify the saved file.

What should I inspect after redacting?

Search for the removed terms and review page content, metadata, comments, attachments, and OCR layers where relevant.
