What the Overtime Pay Calculator does

Overtime rates are simple arithmetic wrapped in employment rules. Time and a half on 20 an hour is 30; double time is 40. The complication is the threshold and the base: in the United States federal rules generally require 1.5 times the regular rate above 40 hours in a workweek, while in the UK there is no statutory right to premium overtime pay at all — it depends on your contract.

This calculator takes either an hourly rate or an annual salary, applies the threshold you specify, and supports two different overtime rates in the same week, which covers the common case of weekday overtime at 1.5x and weekend or holiday work at 2x.

How to use it

  1. Enter your hourly rate, or an annual salary and the paid hours per week for it to be converted.
  2. Set the number of regular hours in the period and the threshold after which overtime begins.
  3. Enter overtime hours and pick a multiplier, or type your own.
  4. Read regular pay, overtime pay and total gross pay for the period.

Features

  • Works from an hourly rate or an annual salary
  • Two overtime bands at different multipliers in one calculation
  • Custom multiplier for contract-specific rates
  • Configurable weekly threshold, commonly 40 hours
  • Totals for the period, plus the annualised equivalent

Worked examples

45 hours at 20 an hour, 1.5x above 40

Regular pay 40 × 20 = 800. Overtime 5 × 30 = 150. Gross pay for the week is 950, which is 42.5 paid hours in total.

Two bands

40 regular hours, 6 weekday overtime hours at 1.5x and 8 Sunday hours at 2x, on a 25 rate: 1,000 + 225 + 400 = 1,625 for the week.

Frequently asked questions

What is time and a half?

One and a half times your normal hourly rate. At 20 an hour, overtime is paid at 30. It is the most common premium rate in the United States, the UK and Ireland, though it is a contractual matter in the UK rather than a statutory right.

Is overtime pay taxed more than normal pay?

No. In most countries overtime is taxed as ordinary income, added to your total taxable pay for the period. A bigger payslip in a heavy overtime month can push part of your income into a higher band, which changes the average rate, not a separate overtime tax.

Does overtime start after 8 hours in a day or 40 in a week?

It depends on the jurisdiction. US federal law uses 40 hours in a workweek, though some states add a daily threshold such as California's 8-hour rule. UK law sets no threshold at all. Set the threshold in the calculator to match your applicable rule.

How do I convert my salary to an hourly rate for overtime?

Divide the annual salary by the paid hours the salary covers, typically 2,080 for 40 hours over 52 weeks. A 52,000 salary is 25 an hour, so time and a half is 37.50. Some employers use a different divisor, so check your contract.

Should shift premiums be included in the overtime base?

In the US, the regular rate generally includes non-discretionary payments such as shift differentials, which can raise the overtime rate above the base hourly wage. This calculator uses the rate you enter, so enter the inclusive rate if premiums apply.
