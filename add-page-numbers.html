About Add Page Numbers

Printed page labels can differ from the viewer's page count. Choose a format, position, font size, and margin that remain readable without covering existing footers or content.

Steps for Add Page Numbers

  1. Select the PDF you want to number.
  2. Choose Add page numbers to place a centered number at the bottom of every page.
  3. Download the numbered PDF as a separate copy.
  4. Review the page sequence and make sure numbers do not overlap existing content.

Review the result

Check cover pages, contents pages, and section transitions for numbering offsets. Keep the original if another numbering scheme may be needed later.

Frequently asked questions

Can the cover page be left unnumbered?

Many page-numbering tools can exclude pages or choose a starting page. Verify the sequence in the saved PDF.

Why does the printed number differ from the viewer count?

A document may have unnumbered front matter or custom page labels. Compare the printed sequence with the viewer's page position.
