Replies usually take a few days. Bug reports about a wrong result get priority over feature requests, and a report with the inputs included can often be fixed the same day.

What to include in a bug report

  • The page you were using, for example the VAT calculator.
  • The exact numbers or file type you entered.
  • The result you got and the result you expected.
  • The browser and device, if the problem is visual or involves a file download.

Suggesting a tool

Suggestions are welcome and genuinely considered, but the bar is deliberately high: a new tool has to be something people search for with a clear answer, something that can be built correctly in the browser, and something that can be explained better than the existing results do. A tool that merely duplicates another page on this site will not be added — that produces thin pages, which is exactly what this site is trying not to be.

Corrections and copyright

If you believe a page contains an error, say so and it will be checked against the underlying formula or the cited source. If you believe content here infringes your rights, email with the URL and the material in question.

What this inbox cannot help with

We cannot give tax, legal, financial or medical advice, cannot look at your personal documents, and cannot confirm what your bank, employer or tax authority will accept. For those questions the right contact is your bank, payroll team, accountant or doctor — the tool pages name the official source where one exists.

This page is a contact route, not a form that collects your data. Nothing is stored on the server when you send an email, beyond whatever your own mail provider keeps.
