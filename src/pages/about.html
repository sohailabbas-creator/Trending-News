SohailAbbas.site is an independent collection of free browser-based tools. There is no company behind it, no marketing team and no advertising network — just a set of pages that do one calculation or one file conversion each, and explain how they do it.

Why these tools exist

Most calculators online are surrounded by things you did not ask for: a cookie wall, an email capture, an interstitial ad, or a download button that secretly signs you up for a trial. Worse, the file tools usually require you to upload the exact document you were worried about sharing. This site was built the other way around. The tool is at the top of the page, the result appears as you type, and nothing is transmitted anywhere.

How the tools are built

  • Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No framework, no build step at runtime, and no external scripts. That keeps pages fast and makes the behaviour auditable in your browser's own developer tools.
  • Everything client-side. Calculations, PDF merges and image conversions run in your browser. Where a library is needed it is served from this domain, not a third-party CDN.
  • Formulas shown on the page. Every calculator prints the formula or rule it uses, so the result can be checked rather than trusted.
  • Limits stated plainly. Where a tool cannot do something — complex Word layouts, encryption, OCR, jurisdiction-specific tax rules — the page says so instead of implying otherwise.

What is checked before publishing

Each tool page is reviewed against a fixed list: does the arithmetic match the stated formula, does it handle zero and negative inputs, does it behave on a narrow phone screen, is the wording free of guarantees, and does the page make a claim the tool cannot support. Dates and reference tables carry a "last reviewed" line so you can see how current they are. No page on this site promises a ranking, an approval, an income or a medical outcome.

What the site will not do

  • Ask you to create an account to see a result.
  • Upload your files to a server, or keep a copy of them.
  • Run advertising or analytics scripts that follow you between sites.
  • Present a calculator result as financial, tax, legal or medical advice.

Who maintains it

The site is maintained by Sohail Abbas. Corrections and suggestions go to a single inbox and are read by one person — see the contact page. When a result is wrong, the fix is published on the page along with the review date rather than quietly patched.

Questions about a specific number are welcome, but please include the inputs you used: most disagreements turn out to be a different rate, a different counting convention or a different number of working weeks, not a broken formula.
