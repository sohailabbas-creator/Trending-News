What the VAT Calculator does

Value added tax is charged on the price of most goods and services in the UK and every EU member state. The one thing people get wrong is the direction: adding VAT means multiplying the net price, while removing VAT means dividing the gross price by 1 plus the rate. Subtracting the percentage from the gross figure gives the wrong answer every time, and on a 1,000 invoice the error is around 33.

This calculator handles both directions, defaults to the standard rate for twelve countries, and lets you type any rate you need. It shows the VAT amount, the net figure and the gross figure together, so the two numbers you put on an invoice always add back up to the total you are charging.

How to use it

  1. Choose the country so the standard rate is filled in, or type your own percentage.
  2. Select Add VAT if your starting price excludes tax, or Remove VAT if the price already includes it.
  3. Enter the amount you have. The result updates as you type — no button needed.
  4. Read the three figures: net price, VAT amount and gross price, then copy the breakdown.

Features

Worked examples

Adding 20% UK VAT to a 500 quote

Net 500 × 1.20 = 600. The VAT portion is 100, so an invoice shows goods 500, VAT 100, total 600. At the 5% reduced rate for domestic energy, the same 500 becomes 525.

Extracting 19% German VAT from a 1,190 receipt

1,190 ÷ 1.19 = 1,000 net, leaving 190 of VAT. Subtracting 19% from 1,190 instead would give 963.90 and understate the net by 36.10 — a classic invoicing mistake.

Frequently asked questions

How do I remove VAT from a price?

Divide the gross price by 1 plus the rate. At 20% VAT, 600 ÷ 1.2 = 500 net, so 100 of the 600 was VAT. Never subtract 20% from the gross figure — that removes 20% of the gross, not 20% of the net, and understates the tax.

What is the difference between VAT and US sales tax?

VAT is collected at every stage of the supply chain, with businesses reclaiming the VAT they paid on inputs, so it is ultimately a tax on the final consumer. US sales tax is collected once, at the point of sale, and rates vary by state and city rather than by product category.

Are these VAT rates current?

The preloaded rates match the standard rates published by national tax authorities at the time of the last review shown on this page. Rates change — for example reduced rates are frequently used as temporary policy tools — so confirm the current rate with your national authority before issuing an invoice.

Is VAT charged on delivery and shipping?

In the UK and most EU states, delivery is part of the taxable supply, so it is charged at the same rate as the goods. If an order mixes standard-rated and reduced-rated items, the delivery charge is normally apportioned in the same proportion.

Why does my invoice total not match my bank payment?

Common causes are rounding at line level versus invoice level, a different rate applied to shipping, or a reverse-charge note on a cross-border B2B invoice where VAT is accounted for by the buyer rather than charged by the seller.
