PDF conversion guide

PDF to Excel

Converting a PDF table to a spreadsheet can make data easier to sort or analyze. PDF pages store visual positions, so extraction may not preserve the original table structure perfectly.

Guide page, not an active tool: Sohail Tools does not currently process or export files. Use a trusted PDF application to follow these steps.

About PDF to Excel

Merged cells, wrapped text, multiple columns, and scanned pages can confuse table detection. Scans may require OCR before data can be recognized, and OCR can misread figures or punctuation.

Steps for PDF to Excel

  1. Identify the pages and tables to extract, keeping a copy of the source.
  2. Use a converter with table recognition or OCR when the PDF is scanned.
  3. Open the spreadsheet and inspect row and column alignment.
  4. Verify totals, decimal points, dates, and key values against the PDF.

Review the result

Do not use extracted figures in financial or operational decisions until they are checked. Correct merged cells and wrapped values manually if needed.

Frequently asked questions

Why are PDF table columns misaligned in Excel?

The PDF may position text visually rather than store actual cells. Merged cells, spacing, and multi-line entries make automatic reconstruction imperfect.

Can scanned tables be converted?

OCR may recognize scanned text, but table structure and figures still need review. Clear scans generally produce better recognition.
