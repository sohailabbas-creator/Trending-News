Built for people who need an answer, not a sign-up

Most calculator sites ask you to accept cookies, create an account or watch an ad before you see a result. These 20 tools do the opposite: the whole page is the tool, the formula is printed next to the answer, and nothing leaves your device. That matters most for the file tools — merging PDFs or compressing images usually means uploading sensitive documents to someone else's server.

What each page gives you

  • A working tool above the fold, usable on a phone with one hand.
  • The exact formula or rule behind the answer, so you can verify it.
  • Worked examples with real numbers, plus a reference table where it helps.
  • Honest limits: what the tool does not account for, and who the authority is.

No rankings, earnings or approval outcomes are promised anywhere on this site. Ranges you see in examples are illustrative arithmetic, not forecasts.

Common questions

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every tool on sohailabbas.site is free to use with no account, no trial and no watermark. There is no paywall on any result or download.

Do you upload my files or numbers?

No. All calculations, PDF merges and image conversions happen inside your browser using JavaScript. Your files are never sent to a server, which is why the tools keep working when your connection drops.

Can I use the results commercially?

Yes. Numbers you calculate and files you convert are yours to use. The site and its written content remain our copyright — see the terms of use.

Why do different calculators show different results?

Usually because of assumptions: how many weeks are in a working year, whether VAT is added or extracted, or whether interest is calculated monthly or annually. Each tool states its formula so you can check the difference.

Are the finance and health calculators official?

No. They are estimate tools that use published formulas. Your bank, employer, tax authority or doctor is the authority for your specific situation.
