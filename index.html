A practical guide to common PDF tasks

PDFs are useful when a document needs to look consistent across devices. The right workflow depends on what you need to do: rearrange pages, share a smaller file, or move content into another format. Keep an untouched copy of important documents before making changes.

When should you merge or split a PDF?

Merge files when you need to send related documents as one attachment, such as a report with its appendices. Check the page order before sharing. Split a PDF when only selected pages are relevant or when a large document needs to be divided into smaller sections. Always review the resulting page range to avoid leaving out important information.

What to know before compressing or converting

Compression can make a PDF easier to email or store, but strong compression may reduce image sharpness. Keep a high-quality original if the document will be printed or archived. Converting a PDF to an editable document can change spacing, fonts, tables, or page breaks; compare the converted file with the source before relying on it.

Convert Excel spreadsheets and PowerPoint slides to PDF

Before exporting a spreadsheet to PDF, check the print area, scaling, page orientation, and whether columns fit on the page. For a presentation, decide whether the PDF should contain full slides, notes, or handouts. Open the exported document and inspect charts, slide order, and small text. These Office conversion pages are independent how-to guides.

Convert a PDF to Excel or PowerPoint

PDF-to-Excel conversion attempts to map positioned text into rows and columns. Tables with merged cells, multi-line values, or scanned pages may need manual cleanup. PDF-to-PowerPoint conversion can preserve a page as an image or attempt to recreate editable objects; the result depends on the source layout. Review figures and formatting before using converted data in a report or presentation.

Make scanned PDFs searchable with OCR

Optical character recognition (OCR) identifies text in page images and can add a searchable text layer. Results depend on scan clarity, page angle, language, and typeface. OCR can confuse similar characters, so verify names, dates, amounts, and other important details. OCR does not guarantee a perfectly editable copy of the original layout.

Remove pages or troubleshoot a damaged PDF

Before removing PDF pages, save a copy and confirm the exact viewer page numbers; printed labels may differ. If a PDF will not open, first try a fresh download, another trusted viewer, and a backup copy. Avoid uploading confidential files to unknown repair sites, and do not assume recovery tools can restore every damaged document.

Save a web page as a PDF

A browser’s Print or Save as PDF option can create a shareable snapshot of a web page. Use print preview to check page breaks, background graphics, headers, and navigation elements. A saved PDF may not include interactive content, content loaded after printing, or future updates to the original page.

Protecting sensitive documents

Use a strong, unique password when a document needs access protection, and share the password through a separate trusted channel. Password protection is not a substitute for checking who can access a file. Before sharing a document, review its pages and metadata for information you did not intend to disclose.

Rotate, edit, number, or watermark PDF pages

Rotate only pages that display in the wrong direction; some landscape pages are intentional. Text edits can shift the layout, while comments and highlights are annotations rather than changes to the underlying text. Page numbers should not cover existing footers, and watermarks should remain visible without obscuring important content.

Sign, unlock, and redact PDFs carefully

A typed or drawn signature is different from a certificate-based digital signature, so confirm which format a recipient accepts. Only remove access restrictions on documents you own or are authorized to modify. For redaction, use a dedicated redaction feature: drawing a box over sensitive text may leave the original content searchable or extractable.

How to choose a PDF workflow

  • To send several related files together, prepare them in the intended order before merging.
  • To share only part of a document, identify the exact page range before splitting.
  • To reduce an attachment size, compare readability after compression at normal zoom.
  • To reuse text in another application, proofread the converted document for layout changes.

Frequently asked questions

Which PDF actions can I do with Sohail Tools?

You can merge PDFs, extract page ranges, reorder or rotate pages, remove pages, add page numbers, and create a PDF from JPG or PNG images. Other pages provide practical instructions for workflows that are not active tools here.

Are my PDF files uploaded?

No. Active file tools process the selected document in your browser and do not send its contents to Sohail Tools. The PDF library is loaded from a public CDN; see the privacy information for details.

Will compressing a PDF reduce its quality?

It can. The result depends on the compression method and document contents. Keep the original and inspect the compressed copy, especially if it contains small text or detailed images.

Why can a converted PDF look different?

PDF and word-processing formats handle fonts, page layout, and images differently. Page breaks, tables, and spacing may shift during conversion, so proofread the output before using or sharing it.

Can OCR make every scanned PDF fully editable?

OCR can recognize text, but accuracy varies with image quality, layout, and language. It may not recreate the original formatting, so proofread important content and keep the source scan.

Why can spreadsheet tables change when converted?

PDFs position text visually rather than storing every table as spreadsheet cells. Merged cells, wrapped text, and multiple columns can make extraction imperfect; verify totals and row alignment.

Is PDF repair guaranteed to recover a damaged file?

No. Recovery depends on the type and extent of damage. Keep the original file, try a backup or fresh download, and use trusted software before considering any repair service.
