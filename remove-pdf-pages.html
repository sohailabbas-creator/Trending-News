About Remove PDF Pages

Printed page labels may not match the PDF viewer's position. A page may also contain information needed for context, signatures, or print layout, so check the document's purpose first.

Steps for Remove PDF Pages

  1. Select the PDF and confirm the page numbers to remove.
  2. Enter individual pages or ranges, such as 2, 5-7.
  3. Choose Remove selected pages to create a new copy.
  4. Download the result and verify the remaining pages and page count.

Review the result

Keep the source until the revised PDF has been checked and accepted. For sensitive documents, confirm removed content is not present in the output's text or attachments.

Frequently asked questions

Can removed pages be restored?

They may be recoverable from the original or backups, but a saved edited copy may not retain them. Preserve the source file before making changes.

How do I avoid removing the wrong page?

Compare viewer page numbers with printed labels and inspect page thumbnails before saving. Review adjacent pages in the final copy.
