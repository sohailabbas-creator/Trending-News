Sign PDF

A PDF signature may be a drawn or typed mark, or a certificate-based digital signature. These approaches provide different kinds of evidence about signer identity and later changes.

Guide page, not an active tool: Sohail Tools does not currently process or export files. Use a trusted PDF application to follow these steps.

About Sign PDF

Confirm which signature format the recipient accepts. Requirements vary by organization and jurisdiction; this guide is general information, not legal advice.

Steps for Sign PDF

  1. Read the complete document and confirm it is the intended version.
  2. Choose a signing method accepted by the recipient.
  3. Place the signature in the designated area and save a separate copy.
  4. Reopen the result and check the mark or signature validation details.

Review the result

Keep the signed copy and any confirmation record. Editing a digitally signed PDF afterward may affect its validation status.

Frequently asked questions

Is a typed name the same as a digital signature?

No. A typed name is a visible electronic mark; a certificate-based digital signature uses cryptographic mechanisms. Acceptance depends on the recipient and applicable requirements.

Can I edit a PDF after signing?

Changes can affect signature validation or document integrity. Keep the signed copy unchanged and use the approved process for corrections.
