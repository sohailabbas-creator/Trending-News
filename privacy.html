Privacy

Privacy policy

Sohail Tools is designed to provide useful PDF actions without sending your document contents to our servers.

Files and browser processing

The supported PDF tools process files you select in your own browser. The contents of those files are not uploaded to Sohail Tools. Processing happens only after you choose a file and start a tool. Keep your original files because the downloaded result is a separate copy.

Third-party library

Active PDF tools load the PDF-lib JavaScript library from jsDelivr, a public content delivery network. Your browser makes a request to that provider to download the library. Your selected PDF or image contents are not included in that request.

Advertising, analytics, and cookies

Sohail Tools displays no advertisements and does not include advertising or analytics scripts. The site does not set cookies for advertising or analytics. Your browser or hosting provider may independently retain standard technical request logs.

External websites

Links to third-party websites take you away from Sohail Tools. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

Changes to this policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026. This page will be revised if file handling, analytics, or advertising practices change.
