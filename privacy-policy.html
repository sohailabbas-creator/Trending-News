Privacy policy

This policy describes what actually happens on this site. It is written to be read, not to satisfy a template. Last reviewed 2026.

The short version

  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts are loaded.
  • Files you open in the PDF and image tools are never uploaded — they are read into your browser's memory and discarded when you close the tab.
  • The only personal data this site ever receives is an email you choose to send.

Files and text you enter

All processing happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. A PDF you merge, an image you compress or a JSON document you format is never transmitted to this server or to any third party. You can confirm this yourself: open your browser's developer tools, switch to the Network tab, and use any tool. You will not see a request carrying your file or your text.

Numbers you type into a calculator are not stored either. There is no database, no session and no local storage entry holding your inputs. Closing or reloading the page clears everything.

Cookies and local storage

This site sets no cookies and writes nothing to local storage. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to. If that ever changes — for example if advertising is added — this policy will be updated first and a consent mechanism added before any such script loads.

Server logs

Like any website, the hosting provider records standard technical information when it serves a page: the requested URL, the time, a truncated IP address and the browser's user agent. This is used for security and for keeping the site online. It is not combined with anything else and is not used to build a profile of you. Hosting logs are retained by the hosting provider under their own policy.

Email

If you email the address on the contact page, your message and address are kept only long enough to reply and to fix whatever was reported. They are not added to a mailing list, because there is no mailing list. You can ask for a message to be deleted once the issue is resolved.

External links

Some pages link to official sources such as tax authorities or standards bodies. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy stops at the moment you leave this domain.

Children

The tools are general-purpose utilities and are not directed at children. No data is collected from anyone, so no special provisions for children's data are required beyond that.

Your rights

Because the site does not collect personal data, there is normally nothing to access, correct or delete. If you have sent an email and want it removed, ask and it will be deleted. Under the GDPR and similar regimes you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the review date at the top changes with it. Material changes — anything that would involve collecting data where none was collected before — will be described here rather than made silently.

Questions about this policy: hello@sohailabbas.site.
